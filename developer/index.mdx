The Developer section is for teams building and maintaining integrations with Centra.

## API support

Developer-facing setup commonly starts with:

- backend Swagger at `/docs` and OpenAPI JSON at `/doc`
- bridge Swagger at `/docs` and OpenAPI JSON at `/doc`
- bridge credentials under `/bridge-credentials`
- bridge integrations under `/bridge-integrations`
- app credentials under `/apps`
